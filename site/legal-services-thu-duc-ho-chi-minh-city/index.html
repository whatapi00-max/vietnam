Practical Legal Support for Foreign Investors, Manufacturers and International Businesses

Thu Duc City has become one of Vietnam’s most important destinations for innovation, advanced manufacturing and foreign direct investment (FDI). Home to Saigon Hi-Tech Park (SHTP), major universities, technology companies and international logistics corridors, the city continues to attract investors from Germany, Europe, Australia, Korea, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong and North America.

TTH advises foreign investors throughout the complete investment lifecycle — from initial market entry and company establishment to factory operations, corporate governance, employment compliance and cross-border business expansion. The questions we answer daily mirror what CEOs, CFOs and General Counsel ask before investing in Vietnam.

Rather than providing isolated legal opinions, our team focuses on practical execution, helping clients reduce regulatory risk while maintaining commercial momentum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign investors establish a wholly-owned company in Vietnam?

In many sectors, yes. The investment structure depends on applicable foreign ownership conditions and sector-specific regulations.

Do I need both an IRC and ERC?

Most greenfield foreign investment projects require both an Investment Registration Certificate and an Enterprise Registration Certificate. The sequence and interaction between the two are explained in our IRC vs ERC guide.

Can TTH assist with factory setup?

Yes. We coordinate investment licensing, industrial land due diligence, manufacturing compliance, labour, tax and post-licensing implementation — see our complete legal and investment guide for factory setup in Vietnam.

Do you advise international companies?

Yes. TTH regularly advises international manufacturers, European corporates and multinational groups. .

Can you assist after incorporation?

Yes. Many clients engage us for ongoing legal support, corporate governance, employment, tax coordination and compliance management. Our practical guide on how to incorporate a business in Vietnam outlines what happens after licensing.
