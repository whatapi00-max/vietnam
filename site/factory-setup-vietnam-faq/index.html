Part of our guide: Manufacturing Investment in Vietnam

Foreign manufacturers planning a factory in Vietnam tend to ask the same one hundred questions, in roughly the same order: whether the location makes sense, which entity to form, where to put the plant, which approvals come first, what the factory must have before it can operate, how it is taxed, how it hires, how it imports and exports, what it must keep filing after licensing, and how to manage risk or exit. We have organised those questions into ten sections. Use this page as a map: pick the question that matches your stage and follow the link to the full answer.

Each section is a standalone article with its own review date. If you are at the start of a project, read Section I and Section II first, then the Complete Legal & Investment Guide. If you already hold an IRC and ERC, go to Section IX and the 20 steps before first production.
