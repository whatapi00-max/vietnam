Selected mandates

Six files that show how we work

Manufacturing

German automotive supplier, greenfield plant in the southeastern region

Industrial park lease · EPE status · Southeast region · 2024–2025

The situation

A tier-one supplier had signed a letter of intent on a plot before checking whether the park could support its planned export processing status and effluent load.

What we did

Renegotiated the lease and infrastructure annexes, secured EPE registration, and sequenced construction, fire-safety and environmental permits against the production start date.

Production started on the customer-committed date; duty exemptions applied from the first shipment.

Corporate & M&A

Acquisition of a majority stake in a local trading company

Legal due diligence · M&A approval · SPA

The situation

Diligence surfaced unregistered land use, informal employment arrangements and a related-party supply contract that carried the group's margin.

What we did

Rebuilt the risk register, converted findings into price adjustment and indemnity language, and ran the M&A approval and share-transfer filings.

Closed with a reduced purchase price and a 24-month escrow tied to the diligence findings.

Market entry

European software group, first Vietnamese entity

Structuring · IRC / ERC · data compliance

The situation

The group wanted a delivery centre quickly, but the intended business lines mixed unconditional IT services with conditional distribution activity.

What we did

Split the scope into an unconditional core plus a staged licence for the conditional line, and set up personal-data documentation before the first hire.

Entity operating in nine weeks; second-phase licence added without restructuring.

Employment

Workforce restructuring at a consumer-goods manufacturer

Labour plan · works consultation · terminations

The situation

Headquarters approved a 20% reduction on a European timeline that ignored Vietnamese consultation and notice requirements.

What we did

Built a lawful sequence — labour usage plan, union consultation, individual documentation — and trained local HR on the conversations.

Completed without labour authority sanction or a single claim reaching arbitration.

Compliance & data

Supply-chain due diligence for a German group under LkSG

Supplier audits · contract cascade · reporting

The situation

Twelve Vietnamese suppliers had to be assessed and contractually bound within one reporting year.

What we did

Localised the group's LkSG questionnaire, ran on-site legal reviews, and drafted a Vietnamese-law-compliant contract cascade with remediation clauses.

Reporting evidence delivered on time; two high-risk suppliers remediated rather than lost.

Disputes

Shareholder deadlock in a joint venture

VIAC arbitration · interim relief · settlement

The situation

A foreign partner was blocked from company records and the local partner had begun transferring customers to an affiliate.

What we did

Filed for arbitration with interim measures, secured document access, and reconstructed the diverted revenue through forensic review.

Settled with the foreign partner exiting at a valuation reflecting the recovered revenue.
