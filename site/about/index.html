Who we are

Local execution, international standards

Vietoria (Ecovis Orient Counsel) delivers full-service legal advice with market-leading strengths in foreign direct investment, manufacturing, real estate and technology. Our lawyers and tax experts work in English, German, Mandarin and Vietnamese.

Clients range from German Mittelstand manufacturers and European corporates to Asian multinationals, state-owned enterprises, diplomatic missions and international organisations. We are referred to by international business and diplomatic institutions in Vietnam, are members of EuroCham Vietnam and the German Business Association (GBA) Vietnam, and are listed by AHK Vietnam as a legal advisory and German Desk contact.

Whatever the mandate, the working method is the same: give the client a decision they can act on, a filing that will be accepted, and a record their auditors and headquarters can follow.

FAQ

What clients ask about the firm

Are you a Vietnamese-licensed law firm or a consultancy?

A Vietnamese law firm with licensed Vietnamese attorneys, able to represent clients before authorities and in dispute proceedings, operating as the ECOVIS International member firm for law in Vietnam.

Can we work entirely in German?

Yes. The German Desk is led by the Managing Partner; correspondence, memos and calls can be handled in German, with Vietnamese-language filings prepared by the same team.

Do you cover tax and accounting as well as law?

Legal advice is delivered by Vietoria; tax, accounting and audit are delivered with ECOVIS member firms in Vietnam so a single mandate can cover all four.

Do you work outside Ho Chi Minh City?

Yes. Most manufacturing mandates involve provincial industrial zones — Binh Duong, Dong Nai, Long An, Bac Ninh, Hai Phong and others — and we deal with the relevant provincial authorities directly.

How do you charge?

Fixed fees for defined scopes such as company formation or a licence application; capped or hourly for transactions and disputes. The fee basis is agreed in writing before work starts.
