Factory Setup Vietnam Checklist

Pre-Investment Stage

□ Determine investment structure
□ Conduct market and legal feasibility assessment
□ Select industrial park or factory location
□ Conduct location due diligence
□ Review land and infrastructure conditions □ Confirm current authority structure, administrative location details and filing practice applicable to the selected province, industrial zone and project type.

Licensing Stage

□ Apply for Investment Registration Certificate (IRC)
□ Apply for Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC)
□ Complete corporate setup procedures
□ Open bank and investment capital accounts

Operational Licensing Stage

□ Obtain environmental approvals
□ Complete construction licensing
□ Register labor and social insurance
□ Activate tax and customs systems
□ Register e-invoice systems

Operational Readiness Stage

□ Import machinery and equipment
□ Recruit workforce
□ Complete operational inspections
□ Commence manufacturing activities

FAQ: Factory Setup Vietnam

How long does factory setup Vietnam usually take?

Depending on project complexity, factory setup Vietnam may take several months due to:

  • investment licensing,
  • environmental approvals,
  • construction procedures,
  • customs activation,
  • and operational compliance requirements.

Can foreigners fully own manufacturing companies in Vietnam?

Yes.

Many manufacturing sectors in Vietnam allow:

  • 100% foreign ownership,
    subject to:
  • investment regulations,
  • sector-specific conditions,
  • and licensing requirements.

What are the most important licenses for factory setup Vietnam?

Typical approvals include:

  • Investment Registration Certificate (IRC),
  • Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC),
  • environmental permits,
  • construction permits,
  • fire prevention approvals,
  • customs registration,
  • and tax activation.

Why is location due diligence important in Vietnam?

Location selection directly affects:

  • environmental licensing,
  • labor availability,
  • logistics efficiency,
  • infrastructure reliability,
  • and operational scalability.

Industrial parks in Vietnam differ significantly in capability and regulatory readiness.

Is Vietnam suitable for high-tech manufacturing investment?

Yes.

Vietnam increasingly supports:

  • semiconductor manufacturing,
  • electronics production,
  • automation systems,
  • industrial engineering,
  • CNC machining,
  • and advanced manufacturing ecosystems.
